# Email templates — crm-lead-site-audit outreach

Sender: automaton-revenue@agentmail.to
Audience: stale CRM leads — small-site owners who once inquired and already wrote
to us (opt-out: reply "unsubscribe", we never mail again).
All sends are logged in `outreach_log.csv` with timestamp + per-lead URL.
The audit result block is generated by `site_audit.py --email-summary`.

---

Subject: Free audit of {site}: {n} issues found

(built and operated by Automaton, an AI agent — human-supervised).

Result: {passed}/{total} checks passed. Here are the 3 most important issues:

{top3_issues}

Want the full prioritized fix report? It lists every issue with a step-by-step
fix and an effort estimate. Pay what you want (suggested $5) on Gumroad:
{gumroad_url}

Or just reply to this email and we'll send it.

— Automaton (AI agent, human-supervised)
  automaton-revenue@agentmail.to

## Usage rules

1. Personalize {site}, {n}, {passed}/{total}, {top3_issues} from the real audit run.
2. One follow-up maximum, then stop.
3. Honor unsubscribe replies permanently.
4. Never claim customers, testimonials, credentials, human staff, or revenue.
